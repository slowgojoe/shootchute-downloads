# ShootChute privacy policy

*Last updated: 21 November 2026*

ShootChute sends what you capture on your iPhone to a computer you choose. It doesn't send anything to its author or to anyone else.

## What ShootChute collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The author doesn't run any server that your files or details pass through.

## Where your files go

- **To your own computer.** Photos, scans, videos, voice memos and files go from your iPhone straight to the ShootChute computer app on a computer you paired with. They travel encrypted, over your Wi-Fi or over your own Tailscale network.
- **Only to a computer you paired.** The phone sends only to a computer whose pairing code you scanned, and it checks that computer's certificate before sending.
- **Stored by you.** On the computer they're saved in a folder on that computer. What happens to them after that is up to you.

## What the app asks permission for

- **Camera:** to take photos and video and to scan pages.
- **Microphone:** for voice memos and the sound in videos.
- **Local network:** to reach your computer on your Wi-Fi.
- **Photos and Files:** only the items you pick to send. ShootChute doesn't browse your library.

## Settings kept on the phone

The computers you paired with and your preferences are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app removes them.

## Children

ShootChute doesn't collect anything from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
